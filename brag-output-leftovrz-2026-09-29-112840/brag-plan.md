# Brag Plan: LEFTOVRZ Grails

## What is this app?
A street sneaker store for leftover heat: deadstock, sample runs and pairs that never made the floor, curated as grails, with a drop every Friday at 6PM, a crew vote that decides restocks, and a buy-side ("Got heat sitting in the box? We buy clean pairs outright").

## The angle
Street, not showroom. The video is a sticker-bombed drop teaser: black and safety yellow (#F8C038), the brush-stroke LEFTOVRZ mark slammed in with the site's own red/blue glitch and halftone, real product shots cut out as die-cut stickers slapped on the beat, and the site's own lines as stamped captions. It ends on the next drop.

## Hook (0–3.4s)
Black. The site's outlined kinetic rows slide across ("LEFTOVRZ · DEADSTOCK", "SAMPLE RUNS · NEVER MADE THE FLOOR", "CURATED GRAILS · ELUSIVE STYLES") while "LEFT OVER / IS NOT / LEFT BEHIND." slams in on the beat.

## Key moments
- Drop: yellow floods the frame, the brush wordmark lands with an RGB split and halftone, "[ THE HOME FOR LEFT-OVER HEAT ]".
- Sticker blitz: real pairs slap on, one per beat, name + Rs. price in Space Mono, SOLD OUT / DEADSTOCK stamps.
- Shop by brand: NIKE / ADIDAS / HOKA / ASICS tiles stamp in.
- "NEXT DROP LANDS IN" — the clock ticking, "EVERY FRIDAY, 6PM", "Get the drop list before it goes public".
- Crew vote: "WHICH ONE COPS?" Air Max 200 Winter VS Zoom Fly, bars fill, winner gets restocked.
- "GOT HEAT SITTING IN THE BOX?" — "We buy clean pairs outright." SELL YOUR PAIR pressed.

## Outro
Yellow. Brush wordmark. "CURATED GRAILS · EVERY FRIDAY 6PM".

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic (held to readable)
- Creative direction: "street drop teaser — sticker-bombed, stamped, on the beat"
- Interpretation: hard cuts on the bar, slams and stamps rather than fades, slight rotations, the site's own glitch; every line still holds long enough to read.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080, 30fps
## Duration: ~22.6s (13 bars at 140 BPM + ring-out)

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background #0A0806, surface #100D0A, border #3D372E
- Accent #F8C038 (on-accent #000), text #FAFAFA, muted #A1A1AA
- Display: Barlow Condensed 700–900, uppercase, tight tracking; body Barlow; labels Space Mono uppercase, wide tracking, [ BRACKETED ]
- Brush wordmark + boxed logo (from the page), hazard-stripe placeholders, 2px borders, no radius

## Share copy (draft)
LEFTOVRZ — the home for left-over heat. Deadstock, sample runs and curated grails, dropping every Friday at 6PM.

## Audio direction
- Music: original street/drill-leaning beat composed in code for this video (`work/street.py`): 140 BPM, F minor, a catchy bell hook, sliding 808s, drill hat rolls, half-time snare. Intro filtered with vinyl crackle; drop on bar 2 (the wordmark); stop-down before the vote; final drop into the outro.
- Cue source: the track's own grid (bar = 1.714s, beat = 0.4286s).
- SFX: only real actions (button press, stamp thuds come from the track).
- Audio-reactive: kick drives the halftone / yellow glow.
