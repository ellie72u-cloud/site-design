# Hyperframes Composition Brief: KARGOS — From, To, When

## Objective
Create a short cinematic launch video for KARGOS that walks through the site's main features.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: landscape — 1920x1080, 30fps
- Duration: 33s (longer than brag's 25s default because the user asked for a tour of the main features)

## Source Material
- Project: the live KARGOS artifact (latest version, copied to `brag-output/work/site.html`; repo copy `hybrid/from-to-when.html`)
- Product name: KARGOS — From, To, When
- Tagline / strongest claim: "Tell us when your cargo is ready. We will show you what you can still catch."
- Real UI captured at 2x from the site into `composition/assets/ui/`: departures calendar, sailing cards, filter bar before/after, compare dialog, company profile, printed-schedule sheet, Following, provider panel (cut-offs), placements rate card.
- Rebuilt from the site's CSS: the container-yard hero tile (`assets/yard.svg`), the KARGOS mark and flip-in wordmark, the three-field search bar with its orange trace, the lane headline.
- Copy that must appear verbatim: "EVERY DESTINATION WE LIST", "Karachi · Port Qasim · Gwadar", the Urdu line "کون سا جہاز ابھی پکڑا جا سکتا ہے".
- Real container lines (Maersk, ONE…) are kept out of the featured shots so nothing reads as a partnership; the fictional sample companies carry the UI.

## Creative Direction
- Tone preset: cinematic; direction "night-shift port launch film"
- Angle: a shipper's question answered by the product's own screens, for every region Pakistan ships to, then the provider side (own cut-offs, placements).
- Hook: "CARGO READY." / "WHICH SHIP CAN YOU STILL CATCH?"
- Outro: "FROM. TO. WHEN." — "KARACHI · PORT QASIM · GWADAR"
- Avoid: rates or prices as a shipper promise, invented stats, generic SaaS language, abstract filler.

## Visual Identity
- Background #05080F with the navy yard tile and a blue glow rgba(26,86,219,.26)
- Text #FFFFFF; accents orange #F25A0F, green #35E08A, blue #1A56DB
- Display: Big Shoulders Display 800; body Inter; labels IBM Plex Mono 500; Urdu Noto Nastaliq Urdu (all local woff2)

## Audio
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 at 0.32, fade in 0.6s, fade out over the last 1.4s
- Cue source: bundled preset (109.96 BPM). Locks: 10.93 (ANYWHERE), 13.11 (first card), 31.10 (title). Board flips on every beat 7.09–10.37 (texture).
- Audio-reactive: subtle; bass drives the blue glow behind the frame. No waveform visuals.
- SFX: soft key ticks on the hook, soft thud on the logo, clicks on Search and the filter chips, quiet flap ticks, card slides, soft drops on panel entrances, one bell on the title.
