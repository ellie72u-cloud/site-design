# Brag Plan: KARGOS — From, To, When

## What is this app?
A Pakistani shipping-schedule search: tell it when your cargo is ready and it shows every sailing out of Karachi, Port Qasim and Gwadar you can still catch, with the cut-off, the terminal and how to book — plus profiles for every line, NVOCC, forwarder and customs agent, and a panel where each company runs its own page. It advertises schedules and providers; it never quotes rates.

## The angle
A shipper's question, answered on screen by the product itself, for every region Pakistan ships to. It opens on the navy container yard from the site's own hero and asks the question every exporter asks ("which ship can I still catch?", in English with the site's own Urdu under it). KARGOS then answers in its own UI: the bar, a departure-board flip through the destinations the site lists (Gulf, Red Sea, Mediterranean, Europe, US East Coast, Far East, East Africa) landing on "PAKISTAN → ANYWHERE", the departures calendar and sailing cards with cut-offs, the compare sheet, the printed-schedule layout people already trust, and the provider panel. It ends on the name: From, To, When.

## Hook (0–3.2s)
Night-navy yard of containers drifting slowly. "CARGO READY." types in; "WHICH SHIP CAN YOU STILL CATCH?" rises under it, with the site's Urdu line "کون سا جہاز ابھی پکڑا جا سکتا ہے" beneath.

## Key moments
- The KARGOS box is lowered on its cable and the letters flip in — the site's own intro, rebuilt.
- The three-field bar unrolls with its orange trace (FROM All Pakistan · TO · CARGO READY); the cursor presses Search.
- Departure board: PAKISTAN ---> JEBEL ALI / JEDDAH / MERSIN / ROTTERDAM / NEW YORK / SHANGHAI / MOMBASA, each with its region, flipping on the beat and landing on ANYWHERE — "EVERY DESTINATION WE LIST".
- The real departures calendar wipes in; three real sailing cards (Gulf, Mersin, Mombasa, Chittagong, Umm Qasr) deal in.
- Compare 3 sailings: the route map (Jebel Ali and Chittagong via Colombo), Download Excel, WhatsApp.
- The schedule laid out like the printed sheet.
- The provider panel: every company runs its own page, free.

## Outro / punchline
Logo on navy. "FROM. TO. WHEN." locks on the strongest beat. "KARACHI · PORT QASIM · GWADAR" underneath.

## User flow worth showing
Open the bar → search every destination → see the sailings you can still catch (calendar + cards with cut-offs) → compare and send/download → (provider side) run your own page.
