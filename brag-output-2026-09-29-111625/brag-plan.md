Second run of the feature-tour video (first run: `brag-output/`). Same product story and real UI captures; the change is the music and the edit.

## Tone
- Preset: chaotic, held back — "catchy phonk edge, not all-in phonk"
- Interpretation: every cut on a bar line, hard drops with a white flash and a short camera shake, the destination board flipping on every beat, a punch-in on each scene. Text still holds long enough to read.

## Music
- Original track composed for this video in code (`work/phonk.py`): 130 BPM, C# minor, 18 bars (33.2s).
- 808 cowbell hook (two-bar melody with an answer phrase in section B), distorted 808 bass on an i–VI–VII–v line, kick/clap/hats with rolls into sections, risers and reverse cymbals into drops.
- Sections: intro bars 0–1 (filtered hook) · drop A bar 2 (3.692s, the logo) · section B bar 6 (11.077s, ANYWHERE) · breakdown bar 14 (25.846s, provider panel) · build bar 15 · final drop bar 16 (29.538s, FROM. TO. WHEN.).
