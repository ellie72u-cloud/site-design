# Brag Plan: LEFTOVRZ — The Premium+ Mixtape (9:16 short)

## The angle
Every Premium+ pair is a track on a record. Retro record-store street style: coloured vinyl, taped sleeves, round price stickers,
graffiti tags, halftone paper, a caution-tape marquee and a wooden record crate. Music: JET SET, everything on its measured grid
(~110 BPM, beat n = 0.017 + n*0.5458s; drop on beat 7; big kick every 4 beats; dropout on beat 24, back on 25).
